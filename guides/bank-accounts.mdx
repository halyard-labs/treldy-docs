---
title: Set up a bank account
description: Add accounts and review transactions in Treldy.
---

Open **Accounts** to see the accounts in your workspace. When a connection is available for your institution, follow the bank connection steps from inside Treldy. Use the correct workspace and check which accounts you are authorising before you continue.

For an account you manage manually, add it from **Accounts** and check its name and opening balance before entering transactions.

If a connection stops updating, [contact support](/guides/contact) with your institution name and the approximate time of the last update. Keep banking credentials and full account numbers out of support email.
