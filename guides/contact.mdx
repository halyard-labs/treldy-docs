---
title: Contact support
description: Tell us what you need help with.
---

Email [support@treldy.com](mailto:support@treldy.com). Include the name of your workspace, what you were trying to do and any error message you saw. A screenshot of the relevant screen can help, with customer details and balances obscured.

For account or billing questions, contact us from the email you use to sign in to Treldy. Keep passwords, bank credentials and verification codes out of your message.
