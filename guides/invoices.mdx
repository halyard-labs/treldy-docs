---
title: Create an invoice
description: Add a customer, enter your line items and review the invoice before sending.
---

1. In Treldy, open **Invoices** and choose to create an invoice.
2. Select the customer or add their business and contact details.
3. Enter the invoice date, due date and line items. Check the descriptions, quantities, prices and tax settings.
4. Review the total and payment instructions before sending.

If a sent invoice needs correcting, check its status first. [Contact support](/guides/contact) if you are unsure which options are available.
